# Finding the Oasis at 60

**Author:** Sandeep Mall (@SandeepMall)  
**Source:** https://twitter.com/SandeepMall  
**Date:** March 8, 2026  
**Category:** thinking

---

Most days, my world starts at 05:00. It's the quiet before the 50-to-60-hour work week kicks in. I meditate. I write. I work out or run. Sometimes training for that next milestone. I drop my granddaughter to school and then I am off to the office. My life is a constant hum of activity.

From 10:00 until the early evening, I'm fully in it. But when I head home at 17:30, the pace shifts. I come back to a house full of life - my parents and my children. That evening tea with my parents is non-negotiable; it's my perfect time. It's where I get my news, too. Between them and my Twitter community, I am caught up.

Ask me about cricket or global politics, and I will give you a blank stare. I have zero knowledge of them, and frankly, no time for TV - unless it's my time with my granddaughter. We sit down for Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah, and in those moments, the 'deep health coach' or the 'entrepreneur' disappears. I am just a grandfather enjoying the laughter.

The weekends belong to my family and my mentees. It's a different kind of work, a different kind of fulfillment. And when the house finally settles, I sit at my piano. Playing those slow, classic Bollywood tunes is my way of breathing.

Looking at it from the outside, it's a lot of hours. It's a lot of responsibility. But I have become someone my younger self couldn't imagine. At 20, the dreams were different. They were probably louder, flashier, more about the grind. But now, at 60, those dreams don't fit anymore.

I don't want the noise. I want the intentional, balanced life I teach others to find. I want the 50-hour week because it means I am serving my purpose, but I want the zero knowledge of the world's chaos because it keeps my mindscape clean.

I am learning to make peace with the fact that our goals must evolve. As Derek Sivers said, 'You have to know your preferences well because no matter what you do, someone will tell you you're wrong.'

People might look at a 60-year-old working those hours, skipping the news, and living with four generations under one roof and have an opinion. Let them.

I am not lured back into a grindset that doesn't serve my soul. I am filling my mind with what matters: my family, my health, and the people I mentor.

Outgrowing your old dreams is an evolution. I am as content right now as I have ever been. There's no chasing. No unnecessary misery. Just a very busy, very quiet, very full life.

I hope you can find the same.

## Key Takeaways

- **5 AM starts** — meditation, writing, workouts before the workday begins
- **Intentional ignorance** — zero knowledge of cricket/politics to keep "mindscape clean"
- **Four generations under one roof** — parents, children, granddaughter
- **Evening tea with parents is non-negotiable** — this is his "perfect time"
- **Dreams must evolve** — what fit at 20 doesn't fit at 60
- **Derek Sivers quote** — "You have to know your preferences well because no matter what you do, someone will tell you you're wrong"
- **Not grindset** — 50-60 hours/week but with purpose, not hustle culture
- **Piano as breathing** — slow classic Bollywood tunes when the house settles
