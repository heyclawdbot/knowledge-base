# Agency Over Intelligence

**Author:** Chetan Singh  
**Date:** February 19, 2026  
**Category:** thinking, career, learning-in-public

---

It's easy to write something lazy that you didn't understand. It's very hard to publish it.

The prospect of shame is the forcing function to do a good job.

This will over time serve as a way to remember your progress and what you actually did. It'll become the resume that actually matters.

We live in a world where agency matters more than intelligence, but our systems, protocols and institutions have not evolved to keep up.

I'd bet on this over networking sessions, 100s of unread job applications and wafer-thin pipedream startup ideas.
